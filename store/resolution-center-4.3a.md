# Antwort an Apple (Resolution Center, Richtlinie 4.3(a))

In App Store Connect → App → „App-Prüfung" → abgelehnte Einreichung → „Antworten" einfügen:

---

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for the feedback. We took it seriously and did not just resubmit: build 1.0.0 now contains four features that we have not found in this combination in other journal apps, and the store page, screenshots and review notes were rewritten around them.

1. Time capsules – an entry can be sealed for 1 month, 6 months, 1 year or 5 years. Until the opening day it is locked: it cannot be opened, is excluded from search, "On this day", statistics and export, and appears only as a lock card. On the opening day a local notification brings it back.
2. Voice notes – entries can carry spoken notes (up to 5 minutes each) that are recorded, played back and backed up entirely on the device.
3. Mood year – the "Look back" tab shows every day of the year as a coloured square and derives personal patterns: best and hardest weekday, the tags that go with good days, and the mood distribution.
4. Journal as a book – Settings → Data → "Export as a book (PDF)" typesets a month, a year or the whole journal into a PDF with cover page, moods, tags and photos, ready to print or give away.

The app works completely offline without an account; no entry, photo or recording ever leaves the device. Step-by-step instructions for each feature are in the review notes.
