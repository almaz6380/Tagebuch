Privacy Policy

App “Daily Journal – Private Diary” for iOS, Android and web · Last updated: October 27, 2026

In short

Your entries belong to you and stay on your device. The app has no account and no server; we never receive your entries, photos or settings. The iOS and Android apps are funded by ads from Google AdMob (section 4), which you can turn off with a one-time purchase (section 5). The web version shows no ads.

2. What data the app processes

Everything you enter in the app – entries, titles, dates, mood, tags and photos – is stored only locally on your device (in the app's own database or in browser storage). This data is not transferred to us or to third parties. We have no access to it.

Your settings (style, reminder time) are also stored only locally, and if you set a PIN, its salted hash is kept in the device's keychain or keystore. The PIN itself is not stored.

If you delete the app or your browser data, all entries are gone for good. You can back them up under “Settings → Data → Back up”. You create the backup file yourself and decide where to keep it.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / camera – only when you add a photo to an entry. Pictures are resized and stored locally in the app.
  • Face ID / fingerprint – only if you turn on unlocking with it. The check is done by the operating system; the app only learns “successful” or “not successful”, never any biometric data.
  • Tracking (iOS only) – iOS asks whether the app may recognize your device for personalized ads. If you decline, you only see non-personalized ads.
  • Notifications – only for the daily reminder you turn on yourself. It is scheduled locally on the device, not sent from a server.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The iOS and Android apps show banner ads and occasionally a full-screen ad after an entry is saved. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google processes in particular the device's advertising ID (IDFA or Google advertising ID), the IP address, device and app information, and information about ads shown and tapped – to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. Data may also be transferred to the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Your journal entries, photos and tags never reach Google.

On first start, the app asks for your consent in the EEA, Switzerland and the UK (Google consent dialog, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021). Without consent to personalization you only see non-personalized ads. On iOS, the system also asks for permission to track. You can change your choice at any time: in the app under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy settings, or in iOS under Settings → Privacy → Tracking; you can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. Personalized ads require that you are old enough to consent yourself (14 in Austria, 16 in Germany). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. “Remove ads” purchase

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple or Google; we receive no payment data. The app only stores locally on the device that the purchase was made, and asks the store for it when restoring.

6. App stores and website

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data under their own privacy policies; we have no influence on this.

The web version is served by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare processes technically necessary connection data (e.g. IP address, time, requested file) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The web version uses no cookies and no analytics or advertising tools.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. We do not store any personal data about you ourselves; for advertising data, contact Google or reset your advertising ID. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (see section 4). You can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).

8. Children and teenagers

The app is not directed at children under 13. Anyone not yet old enough to consent to personalized ads only sees non-personalized ads if they decline in the consent dialog.

9. Changes

If the app ever changes so that further data leaves the device, we will update this policy beforehand.
