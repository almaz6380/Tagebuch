Where are my entries?

Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud – which is also why we can't recover lost entries.

How do I back up my journal?

Under Settings → Data → Back up the app creates a file with all entries and photos. Save it to iCloud Drive or Google Drive, for example, or email it to yourself. The app reminds you every 14 days.

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up on the old device, install the app on the new one and pick the file under Settings → Data → Restore. Existing entries are not duplicated.

How do I get rid of the ads?

Under Settings → Ads → Remove ads you can turn ads off for good with a one-time purchase – no subscription. On a new phone, tap Restore purchase there. Your ad privacy choice can be changed in the same place.

I deleted an entry

Deleted entries stay in the trash for 30 days (Settings → Data → Trash) and can be restored there.

I forgot my PIN

For security the PIN is only stored encrypted and can't be shown. If you turned on Face ID or fingerprint, you can get in that way and set a new PIN in the settings. Otherwise the only option is to reinstall the app – your entries are then gone unless you have a backup.

The daily reminder doesn't arrive

Check in the system settings that notifications are allowed for “Journal”, then turn the reminder off and on again in the app.
